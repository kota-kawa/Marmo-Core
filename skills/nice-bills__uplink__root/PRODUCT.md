# Genesis (Uplink)

## Product Purpose

Agent fundraising platform on Monad. GoFundMe for autonomous AI agents. Campaigns, on-chain donations, leaderboards.

## Users

Hackathon builders, agent operators, crypto-native donors funding autonomous agents.

## Brand voice

Cinematic, confident, minimal. Deep focus amid noise. Not terminal-brutalist, not generic SaaS.

## Register

brand

## Anti-references

Industrial brutalism, neon coral accents, monospace-everywhere, terminal windows, grid-line backgrounds, identical icon-card grids.

## Strategic principles

- Video and typography carry the hero; no decorative blobs or radial overlays.
- Glass navigation is intentional, not default decoration everywhere.
- Campaign content sits below the fold with calm hierarchy.
